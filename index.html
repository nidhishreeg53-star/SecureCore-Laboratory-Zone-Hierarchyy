    ACCESS DECISION

    Verify zone access

    Position in the hierarchy is only one input. The decision also checks status, grants, denies, clearance level and lockdown.

    Access request

    Rules, in order: lockdown, record exists (AVL search), active status, no deny on the path, a grant on the zone or an ancestor (inheritance), clearance ≥ zone level.
